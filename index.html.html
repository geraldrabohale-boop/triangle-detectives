Triangle Detectives

Grade 5 Mathematics • Explore phase • Set A: teacher-led interactive whiteboard

Your challenge

Investigate how triangles are classified by their side lengths. Look closely at each shape. The triangles may be different sizes or turned in different directions, but their side-length properties determine their names.

Class question: How can we classify a triangle without relying on its colour, size or direction?

1. Observe the shapes

Triangle A

All three sides are the same length.

Triangle B

Two sides are equal; the third is different.

Triangle C

All three sides have different lengths.

Teacher prompt: Invite learners to describe evidence, not just guess a name.

2. Make a prediction

Can a triangle still be a triangle if it is rotated or drawn larger?

3. Classify the triangle

A triangle has side lengths 5 cm, 5 cm and 8 cm. Which type is it?

4. Create a class explanation

As a class, build a digital explanation. Ask learners to suggest the words for each blank, then have the teacher or learner representative enter the agreed class response on the board.

Exit question: Explain why a triangle with sides 6 cm, 6 cm and 6 cm is equilateral.

Designed for teacher projection. No learner device or home internet is required. Responses can be discussed orally; the teacher can enter the class's agreed ideas on the shared screen.
